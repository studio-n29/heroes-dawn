# Illustration prompts — board backgrounds (zones)

One block per zone background: copy the whole block (prompt + negative prompt) and paste it into the generator. Each zone has its own place, atmosphere and color palette.

## Format

- **Ratio about 3:1** (target file 3060 × 1000 px). Generate in the widest ratio your tool allows (3:1 or 21:9), then crop or extend to 3060 × 1000.
- **High-angle top-down view**, the **center must stay open and low in detail** so the board squares and the path stay readable; sky and distant skyline only along the top edge.
- No characters and no text in the image: the game draws the squares, pawns and labels on top.
- Save each image as `<zone id>_bg.jpg` (for example `zone_ny_1_1_bg.jpg`) in `03_Art_UI_UX/Illustrations/Plateaux/`. The id in each title is the short form: New York = `ny_<chapter>_<zone>`, so `ny_1_1` becomes the existing game zone `zone_ny_1_1`.
- The difficult version of a zone reuses the same background (it is darkened in the game), so there is nothing extra to generate.

New York and Moscow follow the four chapters of the narration (Brooklyn, Queens, Harlem, Manhattan; Sergueiv, Kapotnya, Sadovoye Koltso, Red Square), three zones per chapter. The New York zones come from the zone list in `02_Lore_Narration/Archive_v1/03_New_York.md` (the fifth act was merged into Harlem and Manhattan); the Moscow zones, Paris and Salem are proposals to validate with the level design.

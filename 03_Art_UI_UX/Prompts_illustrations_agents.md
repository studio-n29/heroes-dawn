# Illustration prompts — all agents (32 playable + 10 Salem cryptides)

One block per agent: copy the whole block (prompt + negative prompt) and paste it into the generator. Each agent has its own look (taken from the lore: appearance, outfit, power), its own location, its own atmosphere and its own color palette tied to the power; characters are shown backlit as strong semi-silhouettes so the costume and the power stay readable while the face stays in shadow. Style and framing are shared.

The golden four-pointed star is the emblem of The Dawn (on the Agents' outfits). Cryptides have no emblem.

Save each image as `<agent id>.jpg` (id shown in the title) in `03_Art_UI_UX/Illustrations/Agents/`. The 32 playable agents use the game ids from `agents.json`; the 10 cryptides are lore-only enemies (S1–S10).

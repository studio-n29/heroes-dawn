# Illustration prompts — Apex Unit and key characters

One block per image: copy the whole block (prompt + negative prompt) and paste it into the generator. The four members of the Apex Unit (Adam West, Brie Rayner, John Guerin, Erik Bronson) each get three images: 1981, 2026 and a power shot. Then two team portraits, and the people around them (Khost, Volkov, Orik, Mephisto, Eda in his three forms, General Stroke).

Each block has its own character, location, atmosphere and color palette tied to the power. Appearances come from the character sheets of `02_Lore_Narration/05_Cinematique_Lancement.md`. The Apex Unit members look the same in 2026 as in 1981 (they have not aged, as in the cinematic). General Stroke has no appearance in the lore yet: his look here is a proposal.

Save each image as `<id>.jpg` (id shown in the title) in `03_Art_UI_UX/Illustrations/Apex_Unit/`.

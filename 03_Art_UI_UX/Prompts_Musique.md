## 1. Thème principal (connexion)
```
Epic cinematic superhero main theme for a video game title screen, "Heroes Dawn".
Mood: heroic, hopeful, awe-inspiring, a new dawn rising over a wounded city at night.
Tempo 88 BPM, key of D minor resolving to D major in the climax.
Structure (about 2:30, seamless loop back to the intro):
- Intro: low sustained strings, distant city ambience, soft choir "ooh", a solo French horn states the main leitmotif: a rising perfect fifth then a major second (D–A–B), slow and noble.
- Build: ostinato staccato strings, taiko and low toms, brass swells, tension rising like a storm gathering above Manhattan.
- Main theme: full orchestra plus epic choir, soaring horns and trumpets play the leitmotif in D major, hybrid synth bass pulses underneath.
- Outro: a single piano and high strings echo the leitmotif quietly, ending on an unresolved chord that loops back to the intro.
Instrumentation: symphonic orchestra, epic mixed choir (vowels only), brass, taiko, cinematic percussion, subtle hybrid synth pulses, piano.
Style references: modern Marvel and DC film scores, trailer music, Hans Zimmer meets Alan Silvestri.
Avoid: vocals with lyrics, EDM drops, dubstep, lo-fi, guitar solos, cheesy 80s synths.
```

## 2. QG
```
Calm, warm ambient orchestral theme for a superhero headquarters hub menu in a video game.
Setting: a converted warehouse in Red Hook, Brooklyn, at night; heroes rest, train and plan; a safe haven in a chaotic city.
Mood: reassuring, focused, quietly hopeful; tension present underneath but kept far away.
Tempo 72 BPM, key of D major with gentle modal shifts to B minor. About 3:00, seamless loop.
Soft felt piano plays a slowed-down variation of the leitmotif (D–A–B); warm string pads; muted horns and a cello; subtle electronic pulse and brushed percussion.
Style references: XCOM 2 and Mass Effect hub themes, Marvel's Spider-Man quiet moments.
Avoid: big drums, choir crescendos, aggressive brass, vocals, sudden loud hits.
```

## 3. Plateau / expédition
```
Atmospheric exploration theme for a superhero tactical game campaign map.
Setting: night streets and docks of New York (Red Hook) under a violet supernatural fog from a cosmic breach in the sky.
Mood: curious, tense but adventurous, urban noir mixed with superhero hope.
Tempo 96 BPM, key of D Dorian. About 3:00, seamless loop.
Pizzicato walking rhythm, muted trip-hop breakbeat, upright or synth bass, noir clarinet or muted trumpet; strings and horns briefly hint at the leitmotif; eerie violet textures (reversed piano, glassy pads).
Style references: Batman: The Animated Series, Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse, Daredevil, XCOM exploration music.
Avoid: full epic choir, constant loud drums, lyrics, happy pop, chiptune.
```

## 4. Combat
```
Intense hybrid orchestral battle music for a turn-based superhero tactical combat in a video game.
Setting: a metahuman squad fights gangs and a colossal brute on the Red Hook docks at night; ice, fire, telekinesis and super-strength collide.
Mood: urgent, powerful, heroic determination; focused enough for tactical thinking.
Tempo 140 BPM, key of D minor. About 2:30, seamless loop, no fade-out.
Brass stab and taiko opening, aggressive ostinato strings, distorted hybrid synth bass, punchy drums; heroic horns play the leitmotif in a fierce minor version; short breather with risers, then back.
Style references: Avengers battle themes, Marvel's Spider-Man combat music, XCOM 2 combat.
Avoid: lyrics, slow passages longer than 15 seconds, lo-fi, ambient drones, dubstep wobble.
```
